Player and button layout

Click an action, then press a gamepad button (you may hold a modifier) or a key. Profiles are saved only in this browser.

DualSense in the menu: D-pad ←/→ selects a combo, Options starts the round.

  • Left stick — move the fighter
  • L1 — slide in the stick direction; a kick right after a slide has longer reach
  • R3 (click right stick) — stance switch: after a rear-leg kick the leg lands in front and the torso turns the other way — you are exposed until you press R3 again
  • Right stick — turn · L3 click — view from behind the fighter

The camera follows the fighter over the shoulder like a fighting game. Click L3 to toggle first-person view.

Poomsae mode: play pose points on a 3D avatar
