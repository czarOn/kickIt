## OVERALL COMMENTS

* **Did the opponent move enough to make you adjust your distance?** Yes  
* **Could you check the gauge before each kick?** Yes  
* **What felt most natural?**  
  The flow from lead setup kicks (like front-leg hook or axe kick) into back-leg power finishers (roundhouse/back kick) felt fluid and realistic to actual Taekwondo sparring dynamics.
* **What felt confusing or unrealistic?**  
  Same-leg continuous combinations (e.g., Push Kick → Crescent Kick with the same leg) felt mechanically unnatural without an intermediate foot placement step, causing unexpected range jamming.
* **What should be improved first?**  
  Implement dynamic footwork step-ins during spinning/turning kick combinations to adjust for push-back distance created by prior strikes.
