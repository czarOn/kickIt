Combination	How to do it
Front leg roundhouse, front leg side kick, spinning back kick	Front leg roundhouse to the body, front leg side kick without setting the foot down for long, then spin into a back kick to the head or body.
Push kick, crescent kick, tornado kick	Back leg push kick to drive the opponent back, step through, crescent kick with the same leg, set it down and finish with a tornado (360-degree) roundhouse to the body.
Hook kick, side kick, roundhouse	Front leg hook kick to the head, front leg side kick to the body, back leg roundhouse to the head: a high, low, high pattern that needs good flexibility.
Axe kick, roundhouse, back kick	Front leg axe kick to the head, back leg roundhouse to the body, then a back kick to the body or head.
Front kick, front leg roundhouse, back kick	Two quick front leg kicks (front kick to the body, roundhouse to the head), then a back kick to the body.
