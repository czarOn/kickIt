Poomsae reference: capture pose points

For a reference, use a camera or a video you have permission to use. Start the capture, perform the movements, then stop and export. Only estimated body points are stored in memory; no video is recorded or sent. The exported points may identify a person. Get a parent's permission before capturing a child. This is not a technique assessment.

Points are not saved automatically. After stopping, choose Export points JSON and check your browser's downloads or Files app before closing this page.
