Getting started

  • Open the game, set your height, choose a combination and start training.
  • Record yourself from the side with your whole body in frame and load the video for analysis.
  • Then get on the mat — this is only preparation for real sparring.

KickIt is an early prototype and training aid, not a technique assessment. Distances and values are approximate and will be refined using recordings. Train safely with a coach. The game and analysis run locally in your browser; your recordings are not uploaded.
